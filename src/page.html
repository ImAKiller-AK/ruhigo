        Legend

        Every pixel is computed from layered simplex noise with domain warping. Zoom in and new detail keeps appearing. Rivers follow a priority-flood drainage model; climate comes from latitude, altitude and rain belts. In the simulation, trade routes are least-cost A* paths over terrain friction, and factions spread along the same cost field from the largest towns, then fight over any frontier that turns hostile.
